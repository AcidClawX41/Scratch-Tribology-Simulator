## Tribology Simulator & Industrial Workshop 2.0.0

**What scratches, cuts, punctures or breaks what — and why. And how a lathe, a grinder and a sharpener really cut.**

Version 2.0, formerly *Scratch Tribology Simulator*, is rebuilt from scratch in **Godot 4.7.2** as one program with two
sections: **Biological tribology** and the **Industrial workshop**. Every number on screen comes from a published model
or measurement, or is labelled as an estimate. Free, open source (MIT), no ads, no data collected and no network
connection needed.

### What's new in 2.0

**Biological tribology**

- **Moving car.** An eagle, an owl, a tiger, a bear or a *Velociraptor* holds on to a car at up to 230 km/h, with the
  wind over each panel from wind-tunnel data. In curves (radii from the Spanish road standard 3.1-IC) each species grips
  harder as its fear grows, slips leaving marks and, too fast, falls off in its own way. Claws scratch the glass only
  when they are hard enough; the windscreen cracks and tempered glass shatters. Optional sun, and an endless road
  between fields, trees and mountains.
- **Falconry Claw Care (3D).** Trim, file and sharpen the claws of raptors, a tiger, a bear and a *Velociraptor*, with
  a tip meter, a magnifier, tutorials and a vet career. An animal held badly strikes the camera, and the lens scratches,
  cracks or smears depending on the claw, its sharpness and the lens you bought.
- **Experimental forge.** Rebuild a broken claw: a furnace with ten sourced recipes (sapphire, ruby, tungsten carbide,
  HPHT and CVD diamond, steel, gold, titanium…), lost-wax casting and a welding table (pulsed laser, micro-TIG and
  silver brazing) with its own tutorial.
- **Claw lab.** The 1.x bench, ported bit for bit, plus Hertzian cone cracks, nail bending, elastic recovery, denting,
  tempered and laminated glass, and body zones down to the bone.

**Industrial workshop** *(work in progress)*

- A lathe, manual and CNC, compatible with the Fagor 8055 T (G41/G42, threading, subroutines): the 3D part is cut
  revolution by revolution, with Kienzle forces and an ISO 286 / ISO 2768 inspection marked out of 10.
- A milling machine and a drill, a sharpener with eleven real machines, and an angle grinder against car body panels.
