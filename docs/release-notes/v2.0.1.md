## Tribology Simulator & Industrial Workshop 2.0.1

The first update of 2.0: **guided tutorials, button by button**, in the forge, the welding table and the falconry clinic,
plus fixes for the moving car, the falconry clinic and the forge reported after the 2.0.0 launch.

### Guided tutorials (new)

With **Tutorial on** and the new **Guided** switch, every action is pointed out: the camera moves in on the object with
a pulsing ring, the side panel scrolls to the button to press («Press here: +0.1 g of tungsten carbide») and a short hint
on the 3D view says what to do with the exact value («Add 0.31 g of WC», «Set the dial to 1450 °C», «Hold on the seam:
7 of 56 spots»). Turn either switch off and nothing is highlighted or moved for you.

- **Forge:** an exact sequence for each of the ten recipes, the bought stone, the living claw and the acrylic (powders
  and grams, furnace mode, temperature, time, pressure, atmosphere, mould, cooling, passes and polishing), and for each
  welding method (pulsed laser, micro-TIG and silver brazing), spot by spot along the seam.
- **Falconry clinic:** a route for every animal (six raptors, tiger, bear and both *Velociraptor* models) that follows
  what happens: if it bleeds, stop the bleeding; if it gets stressed, pause; if it breaks free, pick it up with the towel;
  if something needs fixing (heat, a crack, a tip that is too thin), the hint says how. It never gives away a hidden quick.

### Fixes and improvements

- **Moving car:** claw marks only where the claws really touch. Each claw leaves its own print when it lands, parallel
  grooves only when the animal slips, and punctures or petalled holes after a strike, with more detail (marked as game
  visuals). Glass is only scratched by a claw that slips and is at least 1.25 times harder. Bent (gold) and chipped
  (Swarovski) *Velociraptor* claws are now one piece, and the chase camera no longer ends up inside the tail.
- **Falconry clinic:** stress no longer keeps rising when you are not touching the animal, with any restraint; a raptor
  that breaks free can be picked up again with the towel and hood (a 40 % chance of a strike at your lens, a game rule);
  the heat bar explains that heat keeps reaching the quick for a while after stopping the grinder; the shelf and the
  cabinet no longer cover the windows.
- **Forge:** the scale shows the grams of each powder the piece needs, what you have and what is missing, with «Ready:
  load the crucible (L)»; every recipe can be completed with the buttons alone, and each step names the exact dial mark.
